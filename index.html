Why enrichment matters

Dogs are natural problem-solvers. Without mental stimulation, boredom can lead to anxiety and destructive behavior. Pawloop gives dogs a job to do — sniffing, sliding, rotating and discovering — turning mealtime and playtime into a rewarding challenge.

Our mission

To design premium, thoughtfully crafted toys that engage a dog's instincts, strengthen the bond with their owner, and bring calm, satisfied pups into every home.
